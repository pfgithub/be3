# Crazy 8s

A shedding game for two or more players. The first player to get rid of
every card in their hand wins.

## The table

Any number of players may join the table. Once at least two have joined,
any player who has joined may start the game, which closes the table: no
one joins after that, and anyone else who looks on is a spectator.

## The cards

A standard deck of 52: the ranks 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, Jack, Queen,
King and Ace, in each of the four suits clubs, diamonds, hearts and spades.
One deck is enough for four players, so shuffle in a second deck for a
fifth player, a third for a ninth, and so on.

## The deal

Shuffle the decks together and deal eight cards to each player. Their hand
is theirs alone to see. Put the rest of the cards face down as the draw
pile, and turn its top card face up next to it. That card starts the
discard pile, and it is the card to match.

## Taking a turn

The player who joined first takes the first turn, and the turn then passes
to the left.

On your turn you either play a card or draw one.

To play a card, put it face up on top of the discard pile. It has to match
the card to match, either by suit or by rank. Eights are wild: an eight may
be played on any card at all, and the player who plays one calls a suit,
which the next player must match instead of the eight's own suit.

To draw, take the top card of the draw pile into your hand. If it can be
played you may play it at once; otherwise, or if you would rather keep it,
your turn ends there. Either way you draw only the one card.

If the draw pile has run out when you draw, the discard pile is shuffled -
all but the face-up card, which stays where it is - and becomes the new
draw pile.

If there is nothing left to draw and you cannot or will not play, you pass.

## Ending the game

The first player to play their last card wins, and everyone else loses.

If every player passes in a row, nobody can play and there is nothing left
to draw, so the game is a draw.
